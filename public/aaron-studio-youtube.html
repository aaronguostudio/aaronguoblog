Purpose

These tools help Aaron operate his YouTube channels: prepare and upload videos, manage publishing information and schedules, and read channel analytics. They run as a personal workflow with an AI assistant. There is no public sign-up or hosted account service.

Privacy and data use

Google authorization is completed on Google’s own sign-in and consent pages. The tools receive OAuth credentials; they do not collect a Google password. The permissions displayed by Google determine the access granted.

The workflow can access channel identity, video content and metadata, publishing status, and YouTube Analytics reports for the authorized account. It uses that information to publish authorized content, verify releases, and evaluate channel performance.

OAuth credentials are stored in a local credential file with owner-only file permissions. Production files, captions, upload records and reporting outputs are saved in the operator’s working folders. Delivery copies may be placed in the operator’s iCloud Drive. Selected workflow inputs and outputs may also be processed through the connected AI assistant and its tools. Public content is sent to YouTube, the blog or social platforms when its release is authorized.

Saved working files and reports remain under the operator’s control. Disconnecting Google access stops future API access once existing access credentials are no longer valid; it does not automatically delete previously saved files or already published videos. Those must be removed separately where appropriate.

You can review or revoke access in Google Account connections. Local OAuth credentials can also be removed, and automated runs can be stopped. Reconnecting may require fresh consent. Contact Aaron for questions about saved workflow records.

Use information

These tools are intended for Aaron’s own channels and accounts, or accounts whose owner has explicitly authorized the workflow. They are not offered as a public publishing service. A change to OAuth publishing status does not create a public sign-up offering.

Use of Google and YouTube remains subject to the applicable YouTube terms and Google privacy information. Publishing and reporting depend on provider access, permissions and service availability. Automated checks and AI output require appropriate review; they do not guarantee factual accuracy or audience results.
