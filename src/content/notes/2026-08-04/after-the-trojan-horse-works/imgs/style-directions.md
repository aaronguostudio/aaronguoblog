# Style Directions

## Article fit summary

This Note needs one coherent visual world with mythic recognition but modern editorial restraint. The cover should carry human consequence rather than spectacle, remain readable on the Notes archive, and feel related to Aaron's visual family without repeating its current materials.

## Style cohesion decision

| Cohesion model   | Decision     | Notes                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unified          | selected     | One master composition and one material language serve the cover and social derivative. |
| Controlled Mix   | not selected | No body diagrams or typography require a functional variant.                            |
| Experimental Mix | not selected | The Note is too compact to benefit from multiple styles.                                |

## Recommendation

Use **Quiet Paper Odyssey**, based on **Field Signal Editorial**, with the controlled clarity of Editorial Minimal. Preserve Soft Glass Narrative's restraint and human scale, but omit glass, cyan threads, and tabletop staging.

## Aaron's decision

**Agent-selected.** Aaron delegated the illustration choice by asking for the Note to be illustrated and published.

## Prompt implications

- Treat the horse and gate as a small completed mechanism, not a heroic subject.
- Use a broad matte sea and warm paper sky as negative space.
- Make the boat and single warm home opening readable at mobile size.
- Use subtle paper-fiber texture and shallow relief only; avoid dirty halftone.
- Ban all text and every war, film-poster, AI-dashboard, and glowing-network signal.
