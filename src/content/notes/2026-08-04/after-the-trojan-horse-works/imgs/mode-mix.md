# Mode Mix

## Set-level pacing

One medium-to-loud clean cover carries the thesis; its wide calm sea and paper sky keep the visual emotionally quiet. The social image is a derivative of the same approved master, not a separate visual idea.

## Image plan

| Image    | Role                          | Visual mode                              | Style family                                 | Visual weight | Reason                                                                                                        | Reuse candidate? |

| 00-cover | clean cover and social master | Human-Scale Metaphor + Editorial Minimal | Field Signal Editorial / Quiet Paper Odyssey | medium        | The image must hold both completed capability and the longer human return without explaining the entire Note. | yes              |

## Density budget

- Narrative/metaphor: 1
- Light diagrams: 0
- Dense mechanism diagrams: 0

## Style fatigue guardrail

No glass, cyan thread, glowing route, dashboard, or secondary diagram. Three visual groups maximum: gate and horse, boat, warm home opening.
