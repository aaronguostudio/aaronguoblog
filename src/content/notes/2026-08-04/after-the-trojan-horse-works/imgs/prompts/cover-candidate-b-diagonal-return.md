# Prompt: Cover Candidate B — Diagonal Return

## Role

Second refined clean-cover candidate in the selected visual world.

## Visual mode

Human-Scale Metaphor + Editorial Minimal.

## Visual weight

Medium: graphic clarity with quiet emotional scale.

## Style family

Field Signal Editorial / Quiet Paper Odyssey.

## Positive prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium bilingual Builder Note clean cover, 16:9 landscape, no typography
Primary request: Create an original layered-paper editorial scene showing that the Trojan Horse finishes one objective while a human must continue through the consequences and return home.
Scene/backdrop: a slightly elevated oblique view of a vast calm ink-blue paper sea set into a warm ivory paper landscape
Subject: in the lower-left foreground, a compact muted-terracotta gate with a small original graphite-and-olive wooden Trojan siege horse fully stopped at the threshold; a single dark rowboat with one abstract seated human silhouette and a small amber lantern crosses diagonally through the open center; in the upper-right distance, one warm rectangular doorway cut into a quiet terracotta shore, large enough to read at mobile size
Style/medium: sophisticated editorial paper sculpture and clean print collage, tactile but minimal, crisp large shapes, subtle natural fibers, shallow relief, not photorealistic and not cute
Composition/framing: 16:9 with a clear lower-left to upper-right relationship; keep the middle open and spacious; make the boat the emotional focal point, the horse secondary, and the warm doorway unmistakable
Lighting/mood: calm dawn light, gentle long shadows, reflective rather than triumphant or ominous
Color palette: warm ivory, deep ink blue, graphite, muted terracotta, restrained olive wood, one amber light
Materials/textures: matte paper and card, precise cut edges, limited tactile print texture
Constraints: exactly three visual groups; one predicate; original design unrelated to any film; no text anywhere

## Negative prompt

No words, letters, numbers, title, logo, watermark, map symbols, arrows, labels, extra boats, extra houses, armies, soldiers, weapons, fire, wreckage, storm, crashing waves, giant horse, heroic horse, movie-poster drama, actor likeness, Matt Damon likeness, Nolan production design, film still, photoreal travel scene, neon, cyan thread, glowing network, AI robot, dashboard, glass UI, sci-fi HUD, children's illustration, cartoon character, clutter.

## Mode constraints

One visual predicate only: the technical mechanism has stopped; the human journey continues.

## Density budget

Narrative/metaphor; three major visual groups maximum.

## Prior failure to avoid

Avoid the previous Notes' tabletop still life and continuous glowing or brass route motif. The sea itself provides space; do not draw a path.
