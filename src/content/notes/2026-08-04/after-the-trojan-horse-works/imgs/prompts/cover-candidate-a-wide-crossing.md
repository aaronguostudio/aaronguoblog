# Prompt: Cover Candidate A — Wide Quiet Crossing

## Role

Clean Builder Note cover and source master for a social derivative.

## Visual mode

Human-Scale Metaphor + Editorial Minimal.

## Visual weight

Medium: memorable but calm.

## Style family

Field Signal Editorial / Quiet Paper Odyssey.

## Positive prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium bilingual Builder Note clean cover, 16:9 landscape, no typography
Primary request: Create an original editorial paper-relief metaphor for “the Trojan Horse completed the attack; the harder human journey home begins after success.”
Scene/backdrop: a warm ivory paper sky above a broad, perfectly calm matte ink-blue sea
Subject: at the far left, a small muted-terracotta geometric city threshold with an original stylized graphite-and-olive wooden Trojan siege horse stopped just inside it; across the center, one small dark rowboat with a single simplified seated human silhouette and one restrained amber lantern; at the far-right quiet shore, one clearly readable warm square opening suggesting home
Style/medium: high-end magazine editorial illustration made from layered cut paper and shallow paper sculpture, crisp silhouettes, subtle natural paper fibers, restrained dimensional shadows, sophisticated rather than cute
Composition/framing: panoramic 16:9; the gate and horse occupy a compact left zone, the calm sea and sky create generous negative space, the boat is large enough to read on a mobile card, and the single warm opening balances the right edge
Lighting/mood: soft late-afternoon ambient light, reflective, humane, quiet, no drama
Color palette: warm ivory, matte ink blue, graphite, muted terracotta, limited olive wood, one small amber signal
Materials/textures: clean archival paper, dry matte surfaces, subtle deckled relief, no dirty grunge
Constraints: exactly three visual groups; the horse is stopped and secondary; the boat visibly continues toward home; original mythic design with no resemblance to Nolan's film; no text anywhere

## Negative prompt

No words, letters, numbers, title, logo, watermark, map labels, extra buildings, extra boats, extra people, soldiers, weapons, flames, smoke, rubble, ruins, battle, storm, waves, heroic horse, giant horse, movie poster, cinematic key art, actor likeness, Matt Damon likeness, Nolan production design, photoreal film still, generic AI glow, cyan route line, glass interface, dashboard, network nodes, neon, sci-fi HUD, childish storybook style, cartoon mascot, clutter.

## Mode constraints

One visual predicate only: the horse stops at victory; the human journey home continues.

## Density budget

Narrative/metaphor; three major visual groups maximum.

## Prior failure to avoid

Do not repeat the previous Note covers' beige tabletop, cyan thread, brass path, mechanical instrument, or central product-still composition.
