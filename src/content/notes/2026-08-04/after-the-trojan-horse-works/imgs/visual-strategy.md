# Visual Strategy: After the Trojan Horse Works

## Article character

Reflective AI Note: a compact cultural metaphor about the responsibility that begins after technical capability succeeds.

## Reader emotion

Quiet recognition, moral weight without alarmism, and a humane sense of return.

## Existing Notes baseline

The first three approved Note covers establish a calm, premium family. The first two use warm beige tabletop scenes and a cyan system thread; the latest uses a graphite engine, brass compass, and continuous path on forest-green paper.

This cover should keep the restraint, clean 16:9 composition, one readable predicate, and generous negative space. It should not repeat a tabletop, a cyan route, or the latest cover's machine-plus-guidance-object composition.

## Visual risk

- turning the Note into an epic war poster;
- making the Trojan Horse the heroic focal point;
- using flames, rubble, weapons, storms, or dystopian destruction that imply AI is doomed to harm;
- copying Nolan's production design or showing recognizable actors;
- adding too many destinations, symbols, or glowing system effects;
- shrinking the boat and home signal until the metaphor disappears on mobile.

## Argument beat that needs an image

The Note has one visual beat: the horse completes the bounded objective, but the human journey through consequence and toward home continues. Without the image, the Note loses its most memorable distinction between task success and the life that follows.

## Selected concept route

**Agent-selected: A — After the Gate.**

Aaron delegated illustration and publication. Route A is the strongest because it keeps the Trojan Horse as the completed mechanism rather than the hero, gives “return home” emotional space, and creates the largest visual departure from the three approved still-life covers.

Final visual predicate: **the horse stops at victory; the human journey home continues.**

## Recommended mode mix

- Primary visual mode: Human-Scale Metaphor.
- Cover emphasis: Editorial Minimal.
- No diagram, glass artifact, or separate body image.
- One clean master supplies both the site cover and social derivative.

## Cover direction

An original tactile paper-relief landscape, not a film still: a quiet geometric gate and stylized siege horse occupy a small left zone; a calm sea creates generous negative space; one small boat with a restrained amber light moves toward a single warm opening on the far-right shore. The scene should feel reflective, not triumphant or catastrophic.

## What to avoid

No text, lettering, numbers, logos, watermark, actor likeness, cinematic poster typography, weapons, soldiers, flames, ruins, storm, giant horse, glowing AI network, cyan route line, sci-fi glass, dashboard, or extra boats and buildings.
