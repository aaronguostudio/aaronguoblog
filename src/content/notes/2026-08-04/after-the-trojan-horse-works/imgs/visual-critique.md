# Visual Critique

## Set-level review

The two refined candidates share one selected concept and one Quiet Paper Odyssey visual world. Both preserve a single predicate and avoid text, generic AI imagery, film-specific references, war spectacle, and the previous Notes' cyan-route still-life composition.

## Candidate A — Wide Quiet Crossing

**Decision: PASS, selected**

The left-to-right relationship reads within five seconds: the horse is visibly stopped at the city gate, the rower remains in motion, and the warm doorway waits across a calm sea. The horse has enough visual weight to establish the myth without becoming the hero. The broad ivory sky and ink-blue water create generous negative space, while the centered boat and amber lantern make the human journey the emotional focal point.

At mobile width, the horse, rower, and home opening retain distinct silhouettes. The scene has no text, logo, watermark, fake UI, irrelevant symbol, distorted face, or stock-photo energy. Its terracotta / ink-blue / ivory palette is continuous with the Note family through restraint but clearly different from all three approved covers.

## Candidate B — Diagonal Return

**Decision: PASS, not selected**

The diagonal relation between the stopped horse, moving boat, and warm doorway is coherent, and the layered-paper material treatment is excellent. It feels more tactile and spatial than Candidate A.

It is not selected because the fortress wall, two plant clusters, multiple shoreline layers, and larger home structure create extra visual groups. Those details make the composition richer at full size but weaken the one-predicate read on a Notes card. The horse and wall also carry more foreground weight than the Note's human consequence should.
