# Visual Style Brief: After the Trojan Horse Works

## Base language

Depart from Soft Glass Narrative for this Note. Keep its premium restraint, human scale, hierarchy, and negative space while using tactile editorial paper relief instead of glass or softly rendered tabletop objects.

## Post-specific style name

Quiet Paper Odyssey

## Selected style cohesion

Unified.

## Selected style family

Field Signal Editorial, softened into a calm layered-paper landscape.

## Article thesis

Technical success completes an objective; responsibility begins in the human world that success leaves behind.

## Reader should feel

Reflective, grounded, slightly unsettled, and ultimately oriented toward home.

## Human-scale anchor

A single small boat carrying one simplified human silhouette and an amber lantern toward one warm opening.

## AI / system layer

The stylized Trojan Horse is the bounded capability object. It has already stopped at the gate and remains visually secondary.

## Palette

Warm ivory paper sky, matte ink-blue sea, graphite horse, muted terracotta gate and shore, restrained amber light, minimal olive wood.

## Recurring motifs

- threshold: the boundary of the completed task;
- calm sea: the consequences and time after success;
- amber light: human responsibility and home.

## Image set rules

- clean 16:9 cover, no text;
- one left-to-right predicate: the horse stops, the boat continues;
- three visual groups maximum;
- broad calm negative space;
- original mythic artifact, never a Nolan still or production-design imitation;
- boat and warm opening must survive a mobile crop;
- no separate title-overlay thumbnail.

## Anti-patterns

No epic movie poster, actor likeness, soldiers, weapons, flames, rubble, storm, giant horse, heroic lighting, neon, cyan trace, glass UI, map labels, text, logo, watermark, or cute children's-book styling.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] The predicate reads within five seconds.
- [ ] The horse is visibly stopped and visually secondary.
- [ ] The boat and warm home signal are readable at mobile width.
- [ ] The scene is calm rather than triumphant or catastrophic.
- [ ] The composition differs from all three approved Builder Note covers.
- [ ] There is no text, logo, watermark, fake UI, or film-specific likeness.
