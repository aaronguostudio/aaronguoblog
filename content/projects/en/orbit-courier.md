Orbit Courier is a crossing adventure through floating worlds. Drive a ceramic courier across shifting paths, read the route and find your timing on the way to the next beacon.

The first playable route, Morning Crossing, brings together warm dawn light, floating stone islands, champagne metal and a timber-and-glass crossing. Play in desktop Chrome with a keyboard. No account is required.

I developed the game with AI assistance, carrying an early blog demo into an independent project with its own visual direction, driving controls and public website. Sundial adds a rotating sweep and breaking boards; Skywheel carries the courier to upper islands and aerial steps. Warm ambient music and responsive sounds accompany the journey. Three crossings are playable today: Morning Crossing, Sundial Crossing and Skywheel Ascent. Complete each to unlock the next; progress is saved in this browser.
